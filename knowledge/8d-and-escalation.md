title: Procedure - 8D problem solving and supplier escalation
category: Procedure
---
8D steps: D1 team, D2 problem description with data (what, where, when, how many), D3 containment (block stock, sort, 100 percent check), D4 root cause for occurrence and for non-detection, D5 corrective action chosen and verified, D6 corrective action implemented, D7 prevent recurrence (update control plan, FMEA, work instruction), D8 close and recognise the team.

Every Q1 customer complaint and every Q2 with a rejected lot needs an 8D. Containment (D3) is due within 24 hours, root cause (D4) within 10 working days.

Supplier escalation: level 1 is the Q2 notification and 8D. Level 2 starts after two rejected lots within 30 days or any customer complaint caused by the supplier: tightened inspection, weekly 8D review, supplier quality engineer on site. Level 3 is new-business hold and a resourcing decision.

Capability: Cpk = min(USL minus mean, mean minus LSL) divided by 3 sigma. Cpk 1.33 or more is capable. Between 1.00 and 1.33 is conditionally capable and needs an action plan. Below 1.00 is not capable.

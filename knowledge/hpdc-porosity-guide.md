id: GD-HPDC-POR
title: Guide - porosity in high-pressure die cast housings (EN AC-44300)
category: Process guide
---
Material HSG-AC44300 is a motor housing in EN AC-44300 (AlSi12Fe), high-pressure die cast at supplier V300 AluGuss Süd GmbH.

Two kinds of porosity matter. Gas porosity is round, smooth pores from trapped air, die lubricant or hydrogen. Shrinkage porosity is irregular, branched voids in thick sections that solidify last. Zone A is the bearing bore and sealing face. Porosity above 1.5 percent area in zone A is rejected because it causes leaks and bearing seat failures.

Main causes: die temperature too low or uneven (die thermal control unit fault), blocked or slow vacuum valve, too much die spray, wrong intensification pressure, worn shot sleeve, melt with high hydrogen.

Actions: check the die thermal unit and log die temperature per shot, clean and test the vacuum valve each shift, reduce spray quantity, check intensification pressure curve, X-ray 100 percent until 5 lots in a row are within limits.

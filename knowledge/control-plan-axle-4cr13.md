---
id: CP-AX-4CR13
title: Control plan - drive axle 4Cr13 (AX-4CR13-080, AX-4CR13-078)
category: Control plan
---
Scope: goods receipt inspection (inspection lot origin 01) of hardened and tempered 4Cr13 drive axles at plant DE10. Plant-specific demo limits, not a published standard.

Characteristic HV10, Vickers hardness: limits 450 to 650 HV10, target 560. Method: Vickers HV10 following the ISO 6507-1 test method, 5 parts per lot, one indent per part at mid-length. Hardness above 650 HV10 means the part is under-tempered and brittle. Hardness below 450 HV10 means incomplete hardening or over-tempering and low wear resistance.

Characteristic BEND_F, bending force at break: AX-4CR13-080 minimum 4000 N, AX-4CR13-078 minimum 3800 N. Three-point bending, 5 parts per lot. Low bending force together with high hardness points to brittle fracture.

Characteristic DIAM, bearing seat diameter: 8.00 mm plus or minus 0.02 mm for AX-4CR13-080, 7.80 mm plus or minus 0.02 mm for AX-4CR13-078. Laser micrometer.

Capability requirement: Cpk of at least 1.33 for HV10 and DIAM over the last 25 lots. Cpk between 1.00 and 1.33 requires a supplier action plan. Cpk below 1.00 means the process is not capable and goods receipt switches to 100 percent inspection.

Reaction plan: if one lot is rejected, block the lot (usage decision R), create a Q2 notification against the vendor, request an 8D, and switch the next 5 lots to tightened inspection (20 parts per lot). If two lots in a row are rejected, stop deliveries from that supplier until the 8D reaches D5 (corrective action verified). Release of a batch to production needs: usage decision A, no open Q-notification for the material and supplier, and capability above 1.33.
